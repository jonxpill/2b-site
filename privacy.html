2B

A daily drawing practice, for iPad · Privacy policy · 8 October 2026

Your drawings and everything about your practice stay on your iPad. 2B has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and nothing you draw is ever sent to us.

What 2B keeps, and where

Your drawings, photos of drawings you've taken, your books, the names of the people who draw on your iPad, your rewards and your settings are stored in 2B's own storage on your iPad. They're included in your iPad's own backup (iCloud or computer), as Apple's backups normally are. We have no copy and no way to see them.

If 2B's library ever fails to open after an update, 2B keeps a copy aside on your iPad so nothing is lost. Those copies stay on the iPad and aren't sent anywhere.

The camera and your photos

If you choose to photograph a drawing you made on paper, 2B uses the camera (with your permission) or a photo you pick, and keeps the result in your book on your iPad. It doesn't look at the rest of your photo library.

The weather in the window

This is off unless you turn it on in 2B's settings. If you do, 2B asks for your approximate location and sends it to Apple's weather service (Apple Weather / WeatherKit) to get the current weather, so the window can show it. 2B doesn't keep your location — only whether you're north or south of the equator, so the seasons in the window go the right way round, and even that's deleted when you turn the weather off. We never receive it. Apple's handling of it is covered by Apple's privacy policy. You can turn it off at any time, in 2B or in the iPad's Settings.

The daily nudge

If you set a daily reminder, 2B schedules it on your iPad as a local notification. Nothing is sent to a server to do this.

Families

Several people can draw on one iPad, each with their own books. 2B collects no personal information from anyone, of any age, and has no way of contacting the people who use it.

Saving your books

You can save any of your books as a PDF from 2B's settings. Where it goes from there — Files, mail, a printer — is your choice, made in the iPad's own share sheet; 2B sends nothing anywhere itself.

Crash reports

If you've chosen in your iPad's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may pass us reports of 2B crashing, with nothing in them that identifies you. In Apple's TestFlight testing, crash reports and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send reach us too, as described in Apple's TestFlight privacy notice.

Changes

If this ever changes — for example if syncing between your own devices through your iCloud account is added — this page will say so before the change reaches you.
