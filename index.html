2B

A daily drawing practice, for iPad: one small thing to draw each day, kept in a book a month, on a shelf in a room that grows as you go.

Help · Privacy · pillemerjonathan@gmail.com

Starting

Your first book opens with a letter. Turn the page (tap its edge, or swipe), then tap today's sheet to draw. Each day has a short lesson on a note — two, five or ten minutes, or ten, twenty or thirty. When you're done, tap Finish; until you leave the drawing, "keep drawing" takes it back.

New to drawing? Choose "new to it" when you write your name, and 2B starts with four short foundation books.

The room

Things come to the room as you draw — paced by the drawings you finish, never by streaks, so nothing is lost for a missed day. Tap things to see what they do. Your best drawings can be framed and hung on the wall. The desk's drawer holds the settings.

More than one person

Several people can draw on one iPad, each with their own room, books and rewards. Add someone in the settings (the desk's drawer), and tap a name there to draw as them.

Your drawings

Everything stays on your iPad, and in its own iCloud or computer backup if that's on. To keep a copy elsewhere, save a book as a PDF from the settings ("save a book", or "save them all"), then choose Files, mail or a printer — or put a book's drawings "into Photos", each dated the day it was drawn.

Its age rating

2B is rated 13+ only because its art books include master drawings of the human figure, among them a nude by Albrecht Dürer, as you'd find in any museum. There's nothing else in it that would need a rating.

Questions, or something not right

Write to pillemerjonathan@gmail.com — with your iPad and 2B's version (at the foot of the settings), and what happened.
